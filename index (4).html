Part 1 · The grid

Two number lines make a grid

  • x-axis runs across. Right is +, left is −.
  • y-axis runs up and down. Up is +, down is −.
  • Origin (0, 0) is where they cross. Always start here.

Try it. Tap or hover over any point.

Part 2 · Ordered pairs

Reading an ordered pair

Written (x, y). x always comes first.

(3, −2)

x = 3 → move 3 right

y = −2 → move 2 down

Across, then up or down. x comes before y in the alphabet.
